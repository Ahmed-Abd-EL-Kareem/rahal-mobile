About the Heritage

Nestled on the tranquil banks of the Nile, The Gilded Oasis is more than a hotel; it is a portal to the past. Built upon the site of a forgotten nobleman’s estate, the architecture utilizes reclaimed limestone and hand-forged bronze. Every stay supports the Rahal Heritage Foundation's local restoration projects.
